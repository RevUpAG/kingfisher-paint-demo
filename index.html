FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Are estimates really free?

Yes. Send a few photos through the form or WhatsApp, or ask for a visit, and you'll get a free written estimate with no obligation.

How long does a painting job take?

It depends on size and prep work, and your quote includes a clear timeline. As a reference, cabinet refinishing typically takes about 3 days, keeping kitchen downtime to a minimum.

How do you prepare and protect my home?

We cover floors and furniture, tape edges carefully, repair and prime drywall where needed, and clean up at the end — our goal is zero mess left behind.

What paints do you use?

Standard eco latex, premium Benjamin Moore Regal Select, or luxury zero-VOC Benjamin Moore Aura — we'll recommend the right one for each room and budget.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Our cabinet refinishing comes with a 5-year finish guarantee against chipping and peeling.

Can you paint my business without closing it?

Yes. Commercial and retail jobs are scheduled outside your business hours, including overnight shifts, so there's no downtime.

Are you licensed?

Yes — King Fisher Paint Co is a licensed and insured NYC Home Improvement Contractor, license #2049182.
